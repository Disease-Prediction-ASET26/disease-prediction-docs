Project title:
    Disease Prediction App

Description:
    Build an application in which users can describe their symptoms (text/selecting a structured checklist)
    and the system predicts the most likely disease with calibrated probabilities.
    Every prediction should be explained and paired with disease description and general precautions.
    The output of the system can be a list of the most likely diseases associated with the symptoms,
    followed by explanations and a system-calculated score (indicating the probability that a patient with those symptoms has disease X).
